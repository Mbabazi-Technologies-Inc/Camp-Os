3 · On the phone

Most summer staff will only ever use it on their phones. Counsellors get a lighter app: chat, their tasks, Knowledge and People, without parent email or bookings.

1Two kinds of appOffice staff keep every tool. Counsellors get chat, their tasks, Knowledge and People.
2SafeguardingUnder-18 CITs can't be direct-messaged one-on-one by adults. Messages can't be deleted, and directors can see every channel. All of these are settings.
3Season-end clean-upSummer access expires on its own and session channels are archived, so retreat season starts clean with only year-round staff.

4 · Director: send tasks and see the whole team

The director sends a task once, to one person or a whole role, and can make it repeat. Each repeat lands on the right people's task lists by itself. The Team tasks page shows, at a glance, what's done, what's late and who did it.

5 · On the phone: my tasks and the team

Staff open the app and their day is already there. The director can check the whole camp from a phone while walking the grounds.

1Nothing to set up each dayRoutines are set once per season. Summer ones end on Aug 29; retreat-season ones (like "Lodge turnover after each group") take over in September.
2Simple for staffStaff never need to visit Team tasks. They open My tasks, tick things off, and that's all.
3Quick for the directorLate items come first, nudging takes one tap, and new tasks can be sent from any screen, including from a chat message.
